# ScopeGuard Employee Handbook

Welcome to the team! Below are the standard operational guidelines for all ScopeGuard employees. For comprehensive details on your equity grants or specific compensation bands, please contact HR.

### 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)
We operate on a "Flexible PTO" model. We do not track days, but we explicitly expect every employee to take a *minimum* of 3 weeks off per year to recharge. 
* **Procedure:** For time off longer than 3 days, please request approval from your direct manager in Gusto at least two weeks in advance. 
* **Sick Days:** If you are sick, stay offline. Do not push through it. Ping your manager in Slack and rest.

### 2. Remote Work & Core Hours
Our headquarters is located in San Francisco, and local employees are encouraged to come in Tuesdays and Thursdays. However, we are a remote-first culture. 
* **Core Hours:** To facilitate synchronous collaboration across time zones, we expect everyone to be online and available between **10:00 AM and 2:00 PM Pacific Time**. 

### 3. Equipment & Expense Policy
ScopeGuard provides all necessary hardware to ensure you can work effectively and securely.
* **Tech Stipend:** New hires receive a $1,500 stipend to equip their home office (monitors, chairs, keyboards). 
* **Travel:** All business travel (e.g., to client on-sites or our annual offsite) must be booked through our corporate travel portal. 
* **Reimbursement:** Submit all receipts through Expensify. Approvals happen on the 15th and 30th of each month. 

### 4. Code of Conduct
We build security software, which means integrity is our core currency. We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or hostile behavior in any form. Be respectful, assume good intent, and foster an inclusive environment.
