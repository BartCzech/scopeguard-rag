# Architecture Decision Record 012: Vector Store Selection

**Date:** 2023-11-15
**Status:** Accepted
**Author:** Paul Kepovitz
**Reviewers:** Core Engineering Team

### Context
As we build out the v1 of the ScopeGuard middleware, we need to select the primary vector database that our platform will natively support and use for our internal testing benchmarks. We evaluated Pinecone, Weaviate, and Milvus. The primary requirement is the ability to efficiently handle our dynamic pre-filtering algorithm (metadata filtering) without massive latency spikes, as this is the core of our zero-trust RAG architecture.

### Decision
We will proceed with **Pinecone** as our tier-1 supported vector store and our primary internal testbed. 

### Rationale
1. **Metadata Filtering Performance:** In our load testing (1M vectors, dimension size 1536), Pinecone's proprietary single-stage filtering consistently returned results in <40ms when applying complex JSON metadata filters (which we use for scope tags). Weaviate struggled with highly nested scope permissions, pushing latency above 120ms in edge cases.
2. **Managed Infrastructure:** As a lean team of ~40, we cannot afford dedicated headcount to manage vector database infrastructure. Pinecone's serverless offering offloads this entirely.
3. **Cost vs Value:** While Pinecone is expensive (our current AWS/infrastructure spend is hovering around $47K/month, a large chunk of which is Pinecone), the engineering hours saved justify the premium at our current scale. 

### Consequences
* We are locking in some vendor dependence early on. 
* We will need to optimize our caching layer aggressively to keep Pinecone read costs down as we scale enterprise customers.
* *Action Item:* We will still build abstractions to support Weaviate and Chroma in the future (likely Q2 2024 roadmap) to support customers who self-host, but Pinecone is the default for our cloud deployment.
