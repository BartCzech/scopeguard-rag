# Sales Playbook: Handling Competitors & Objections

**Target Audience:** Enterprise AEs

When selling the Enterprise or Business tier, you will face pushback. Here is how to navigate the most common objections.

### Objection 1: "It's too expensive / RivalShield quoted us $10 a seat."
**Response:** Acknowledge the price difference, but pivot to Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and risk. 
* *Talk track:* "RivalShield is cheaper upfront because they are just an API proxy doing post-retrieval redaction. But their approach adds up to 800ms of latency per query, frustrating your users, and fundamentally leaves your data exposed to context-window leaks. ScopeGuard is an infrastructural guarantee. What is the cost of a single HR data leak to your company?"

### Objection 2: "We can just build this ourselves using system prompts."
**Response:** Lean into Paul's blog post (PUB-005). 
* *Talk track:* "Prompt injection is an unsolved mathematical problem. If you put sensitive data into the LLM's context window and ask it to keep a secret, you are relying on a probabilistic text generator to act as a security firewall. We stop the data at the vector database."

### Objection 3: "I heard VaultContext is suing you over a patent?"
**Response:** De-escalate and redirect. 
* *Talk track:* "VaultContext filed a nuisance claim regarding some generic indexing concepts (US Patent 11,xxx,xxx), which our outside counsel expects to be dismissed. It has zero impact on our product availability, roadmap, or your deployment. We're fully indemnified." *(Note: Do not offer further details. Pivot back to the technical evaluation.)*
