# Confidential HR Investigation Report

**Date of Report:** November 18, 2024
**Investigator:** External HR Consultant (Sarah Willis)
**Subject:** Anonymous complaint regarding hostile workplace behavior
**Respondent:** [REDACTED - Engineering Manager, Backend Squad]

### Background
On November 2, 2024, HR received an anonymous complaint via the internal reporting portal alleging that the Respondent has been creating a hostile work environment. Specific allegations included yelling during sprint planning meetings, publicly belittling junior engineers in pull request reviews, and making unreasonable demands regarding weekend work.

### Investigation Methodology
Between Nov 5 and Nov 15, I conducted one-on-one interviews with six members of the Backend Squad, including the Respondent. All names have been kept confidential. 

### Findings
1. **Public Belittling:** Several engineers corroborated that the Respondent's tone during PR reviews is overly aggressive and unprofessional. Remarks such as "this is intern-level code" were documented in GitHub.
2. **Yelling:** Two witnesses confirmed an incident during the Q3 Sprint 4 retrospective where the Respondent raised their voice and slammed a hand on a desk out of frustration over flaky integration tests.
3. **Weekend Work:** Evidence does not support that weekend work was explicitly mandated, but the Respondent sets a precedent by working weekends and expressing frustration when others do not reply immediately on Slack.

### Conclusion & Recommendations
The Respondent's behavior violates the ScopeGuard Code of Conduct regarding professional respect and inclusivity. While the Respondent is technically brilliant and critical to the current roadmap, this management style is driving a high risk of attrition.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Issue a formal Written Warning to the Respondent.
2. Mandate completion of a 6-week executive coaching and management training program.
3. HR to conduct a follow-up squad health check in 60 days.
