# 2025 Strategic Priorities & Budget Allocations

**Approved by the Board of Directors: December 5, 2024**

To maintain momentum toward a Series B raise in early 2026, the executive team has outlined three core strategic pillars for 2025. All departmental OKRs must align with these initiatives.

### Pillar 1: Achieve SOC 2 Type II Certification
Enterprise procurement is our biggest bottleneck. Closing deals like FinServe takes months of manual security questionnaires. We must achieve automated compliance trust.
* **Target:** Audit completion by Q2 2025.
* **Budget Allocation:** $85,000 for external auditors (Sensiba San Filippo) and continuous compliance software (Vanta).
* **Owner:** Paul Kepovitz & DevOps

### Pillar 2: Launch MCP-Native Integration
Standard RAG is becoming commoditized. To maintain our valuation, we must position ScopeGuard as the default authorization layer for autonomous AI agents, not just document retrieval. 
* **Target:** Alpha release by May 2025.
* **Budget Allocation:** Authorization to open headcount for two (2) Senior Backend Engineers focused entirely on the Model Context Protocol (estimated $450,000 annualized compensation burden). 
* **Owner:** Bart Check (Product definition), Core Backend Squad (Execution)

### Pillar 3: EU Data Center Expansion (Frankfurt)
Data residency concerns blocked three major deals in Q3. Expanding our infrastructure footprint to Europe is non-negotiable for 2025 revenue targets.
* **Target:** Infrastructure operational by Q3 2025.
* **Budget Allocation:** Approval for up to $15,000/month in additional AWS spend to replicate our Pinecone, Redis, and EC2 architecture in the `eu-central-1` region. 
* **Owner:** Infrastructure Squad

### Financial Guardrails
While executing these pillars, we must hold the line on our overall burn rate. We are currently burning ~$480K/month with 29 months of runway. Any new software tooling or non-essential T&E expenses exceeding $5,000 must be personally approved by Elena Rostova (VP Finance).
