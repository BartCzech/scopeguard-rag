# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Bart Check (CEO), Paul Kepovitz (Head of Engineering)
**FROM:** Fenwick & West LLP (Outside Counsel)
**DATE:** September 14, 2024
**SUBJECT:** Summary of Executed Series A Term Sheet (Crest Ventures)

This memorandum summarizes the binding terms of the Series A financing round led by Crest Ventures. The final closing documents are currently being drafted.

### Valuation and Investment
* **Pre-Money Valuation:** $58,000,000
* **Target Raise:** $14,000,000
* **Post-Money Valuation:** $72,000,000
* **Lead Investor:** Crest Ventures ($10,000,000 allocation). The remaining $4,000,000 is fully allocated to returning angel investors and a strategic syndicate.

### Capital Structure & Rights
* **Liquidation Preference:** Standard 1x non-participating preferred. In the event of a liquidation or acquisition, Series A investors will receive their money back before common stockholders receive proceeds.
* **Pro-Rata Rights:** Crest Ventures retains the right to participate in future financing rounds (e.g., Series B) to maintain their percentage of ownership.

### Board of Directors
Upon closing, the ScopeGuard AI Board of Directors will be reconstituted to a five (5) person board consisting of:
1. Two (2) seats held by the Founders (Bart Check, Paul Kepovitz).
2. One (1) seat designated by the Lead Investor (Crest Ventures).
3. Two (2) independent seats, mutually agreed upon by the Founders and the Lead Investor.

### Option Pool
The post-money employee option pool will be expanded to represent 12% of the fully diluted capitalization to ensure sufficient equity for hiring over the next 24 months.
