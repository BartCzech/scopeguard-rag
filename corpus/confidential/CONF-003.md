# 2024-2025 Compensation Framework

**Document Owner:** HR / Finance
**Last Updated:** October 2024 (Post-Series A)

This document outlines the approved salary bands and equity targets for all roles at ScopeGuard AI. Our philosophy targets the 75th percentile of SF-bay area startups at the Series A stage.

*Note: All salaries are listed in USD. Equity percentages represent fully diluted shares at the time of grant.*

### Engineering & Product
* **Junior Engineer (L1):** $120,000 – $140,000 | 0.05% - 0.08%
* **Mid-Level Engineer (L2):** $145,000 – $175,000 | 0.1% - 0.15%
* **Senior Engineer (L3):** $185,000 – $225,000 | 0.2% - 0.35%
* **Staff Engineer (L4):** $230,000 – $250,000 | 0.4% - 0.5%
* **Product Manager:** $150,000 – $180,000 | 0.15% - 0.25%

### Go-To-Market & Sales
* **Account Executive (Mid-Market):** $110,000 Base / $220,000 OTE | 0.1%
* **Enterprise Account Executive:** $140,000 Base / $280,000 OTE | 0.15%
* **Marketing Manager:** $130,000 – $160,000 | 0.1%

### Leadership & Executives
* **VP Engineering:** $260,000 – $310,000 | 0.5% - 1.0%
* **VP Sales:** $200,000 Base / $400,000 OTE | 0.5% - 1.0%
* **Founders (Bart Check, Paul Kepovitz):** $195,000 each. *(Note: Founder salaries were increased from $120K to $195K following the Series A close, as approved by the compensation committee).*

### Annual Review Cycle
Merit increases are evaluated annually in January. Promotion cycles occur bi-annually in January and July. Off-cycle increases require explicit board approval if they exceed the top of the approved band.
