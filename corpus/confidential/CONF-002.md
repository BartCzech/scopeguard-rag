# ScopeGuard AI - Q4 2024 Board Meeting Minutes

**Date:** December 5, 2024
**Location:** ScopeGuard HQ, San Francisco / Zoom
**Attendees:** Bart Check, Paul Kepovitz, Sarah Jenkins (Crest Ventures), David Lee (Independent), Elena Rostova (VP Finance - Observing)

### 1. Financial Update (Elena Rostova)
* **ARR:** $1.2M
* **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** 140%
* **Burn Rate:** ~$480K / month
* **Runway:** 29 months (assuming no top-line growth). 
* The board commended the team on maintaining a healthy NRR, driven largely by seat expansion at our earliest design partners. 

### 2. Go-to-Market Strategy (Bart Check)
* Crest Ventures (Sarah) pushed heavily for accelerating enterprise sales. The $15/seat PLG motion is fine for developer adoption, but the real revenue is in six-figure annual contracts. 
* Debate: Do we pivot from being purely "middleware" to offering a full-stack RAG platform (including our own chunking, embedding models, and UI)?
* **Resolution:** Paul strongly advocated against this, noting that our moat is being model-agnostic and focusing purely on the authorization layer. The board agreed to remain focused on the middleware layer for H1 2025, pending the rollout of MCP integrations.

### 3. Series B Timeline
* Assuming a target of $3M-$4M ARR to raise a Series B at a favorable markup, we are likely looking at Q1 2026. 
* We need to start having preliminary coffee chats with growth-stage funds by Q3 2025.

### 4. Legal / HR Matters
* Quick update on the VaultContext patent nuisance claim. Outside counsel is handling it; no material impact expected on runway.
