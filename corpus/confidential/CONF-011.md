# Compensation Committee Meeting Minutes

**Date:** October 2, 2024 (Post-Series A Closing)
**Committee Members:** Sarah Jenkins (Crest Ventures), David Lee (Independent Board Member)
**Subject:** Executive Compensation and Equity Refresh

### 1. Founder Salary Adjustments
Prior to the Series A, Co-founders Bart Check and Paul Kepovitz were drawing below-market salaries of $120,000 each to preserve cash. Following the successful $14M capital injection, the founders requested an adjustment to market rates. 
* **Discussion:** The committee reviewed Series A SaaS startup compensation benchmarks in the SF Bay Area. 
* **Resolution:** Approved unanimously. The base salary for both Bart Check and Paul Kepovitz is increased to **$195,000**, effective immediately. No additional founder equity was granted.

### 2. VP Sales Target Compensation
To hit the aggressive 2025 revenue targets, the company is actively recruiting a VP of Sales. 
* **Discussion:** The proposed compensation band from HR was $180k Base / $360k OTE. Sarah Jenkins noted this is too low to pull a tier-1 enterprise leader from a competitor like RivalShield.
* **Resolution:** The committee approved a maximum band of **$200,000 Base / $400,000 OTE**, with an equity grant up to 1.0% (vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff). The bonus structure will be tied strictly to closed-won Net New ARR.

### 3. Employee Equity Refresh Pool
* **Discussion:** Several key early engineers are approaching their 2-year mark. To prevent attrition, management proposed an equity refresh pool.
* **Resolution:** Approved. 1.5% of the fully diluted cap table is carved out for performance-based retention grants for employees hired prior to January 2024. Grants will be distributed at the CEO's discretion during the January 2025 review cycle, subject to a new 4-year vesting schedule.
