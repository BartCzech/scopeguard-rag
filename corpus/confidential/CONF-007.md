# Q3 2024 Customer Churn Report

**Prepared by:** Customer Success
**Date:** October 10, 2024

### Executive Summary
During Q3 2024, ScopeGuard experienced 4 customer churns. While our overall Net Revenue Retention (NRR) remains extremely healthy at 140% due to seat expansion in our Enterprise accounts, the logos lost this quarter highlight emerging friction points in our GTM motion. 

The total net revenue impact of these churns is **-$18,400 MRR**. 

### Churn Breakdown

**1. Acme Logistics (Business Tier)**
* **Impact:** -$3,500 MRR
* **Reason:** Pricing. They failed to see the ROI of the $45/user/month tier after their initial rollout stalled. They decided to downgrade to standard system prompts and accepted the security risk.

**2. NexaTech Solutions (Starter Tier)**
* **Impact:** -$900 MRR
* **Reason:** Pricing / Competitor. NexaTech actively evaluated us against RivalShield AI. They opted for RivalShield's $10/seat pricing model because their infosec requirements were relaxed enough to accept post-retrieval filtering. 

**3. Meridian Health (Enterprise Tier)**
* **Impact:** -$11,500 MRR
* **Reason:** Direct Competitor Loss. This is our most painful churn. Meridian switched to RivalShield after RivalShield offered them a massive discount and waived all setup fees for an on-premise deployment. Our refusal to support on-premise deployments in Q3 ultimately cost us the renewal. 

**4. DataSync Inc. (Starter Tier)**
* **Impact:** -$2,500 MRR
* **Reason:** Acquisition. DataSync was acquired by a larger conglomerate that mandates the use of Microsoft's native Azure AI compliance tools. The decision was out of the champion's hands.

### Action Items
* **Pricing Strategy:** We are losing the bottom end of the market to RivalShield. Marketing needs to aggressively ramp up campaigns focusing on the latency and security flaws of RivalShield's post-retrieval architecture to justify our premium.
* **On-Premise Evaluation:** We need to reopen the discussion on supporting VPC / On-Premise deployments for Enterprise healthcare clients in 2025.
