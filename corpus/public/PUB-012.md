ScopeGuard is designed to seamlessly drop into your existing AI infrastructure. As middleware, we sit between your identity provider, your vector database, and your chosen LLM. 

Below is the current list of officially supported platforms as of our v2.4 release. 

### Identity Providers (IdP)
ScopeGuard maps your existing organizational roles and groups directly to retrieval scopes.
* **Okta** (Native integration via OAuth2/OIDC)
* **Auth0**
* **Google Workspace** (Enterprise)
* **Microsoft Entra ID** (formerly Azure AD)

### Vector Databases
We intercept the vector search query, apply our scope-filtering algorithm, and pass it securely to your database.
* **Pinecone** (Fully managed serverless & pod-based)
* **Weaviate** (Cloud and self-hosted)
* **Chroma** (Native support added in v2.3)
* *Coming Soon: Qdrant and Milvus*

### Supported LLM Providers
Because ScopeGuard operates at the retrieval layer, we are entirely model-agnostic. Once the safe context is retrieved, you can pass it to any generative model.
* **OpenAI** (GPT-4o, GPT-4 Turbo, GPT-3.5)
* **Anthropic** (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Opus, Haiku)
* **Cohere** * **Hugging Face** (Any hosted open-source models like Llama 3 or Mistral)
