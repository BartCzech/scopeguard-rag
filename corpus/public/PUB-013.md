# Join the ScopeGuard Team

We are on a mission to secure the enterprise AI boom. Founded in 2023 by ex-Google and ex-Palantir engineers, ScopeGuard is building the definitive authorization layer for Large Language Models. 

Following our recent $14M Series A led by Crest Ventures, we are scaling rapidly. We are a tight-knit team of about 40 builders, thinkers, and hackers based in San Francisco (and fully supportive of remote work). We ship fast, we embrace hard technical challenges, and we believe that AI should only know what it's supposed to know.

### Why Work Here?
* **Impact:** You will be solving the biggest bottleneck in enterprise AI adoption—data security.
* **Growth:** Get in on the ground floor post-Series A.
* **Benefits:** Comprehensive health coverage, flexible PTO, and competitive salaries with generous equity packages.
