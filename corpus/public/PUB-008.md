**Securing Client Data for 2,000 Financial Advisors**

**The Challenge**
FinServe Corp, a mid-sized financial services firm, wanted to empower its 2,000 advisors with an internal AI assistant to instantly query market research, company policies, and client portfolios. However, strict financial regulations mandated absolute data isolation. A junior analyst asking the AI for "recent portfolio summaries" could not be allowed to see the private holdings of another advisor's high-net-worth clients. 

Relying on system prompts to hide this data was deemed a critical compliance risk by FinServe’s infosec team, stalling the entire AI initiative. 

**The Solution**
FinServe deployed ScopeGuard AI as the routing layer between their custom LLM application and their Pinecone vector database. 

By integrating ScopeGuard with FinServe’s existing Okta identity provider, the engineering team mapped complex organizational roles directly to AI retrieval scopes. When an advisor queries the assistant, ScopeGuard securely authenticates the user in milliseconds and intercepts the vector search. The Pinecone database only returns embeddings explicitly tagged with that specific advisor's access scope. 

**The Results**
* **Zero Data Leaks:** In over 500,000 queries since launch, there have been zero instances of cross-client data leakage. 
* **100% Compliance:** ScopeGuard’s transparent retrieval logging satisfied FinServe’s internal auditors, allowing the project to pass compliance review.
* **Unblocked AI Adoption:** FinServe’s advisors now save an average of 45 minutes per day on research tasks, safely leveraging GenAI across sensitive financial data.

*"We used to worry about our client data leaking through the AI assistant,"* said FinServe's VP of Engineering. *"ScopeGuard gave us the mathematical certainty we needed to finally push our RAG system into production."*
