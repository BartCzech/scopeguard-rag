**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**ScopeGuard AI Raises $14M Series A to Secure the Enterprise AI Boom**

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA** — ScopeGuard AI, the pioneer in access-controlled retrieval middleware for Large Language Models, today announced the closing of a $14 million Series A funding round. The round was led by Crest Ventures, with participation from several strategic angel investors in the cybersecurity space. 

As companies race to implement internal AI assistants across their organizations, data privacy has become a massive bottleneck. Standard AI implementations lack the ability to enforce strict, enterprise-grade access controls, creating severe risks of data leakage. 

ScopeGuard solves this by sitting between the AI assistant and the corporate knowledge base. 

"The fundamental promise of enterprise AI is that it can reason over your company's proprietary data," said Bart Check, Co-founder and CEO of ScopeGuard AI. "But the reality is that not every employee should have access to every piece of data. With this new capital, we are scaling our middleware platform so that companies can deploy AI with confidence. Our philosophy is simple: your AI knows only what it should."

"Current approaches to AI authorization rely on easily bypassed system prompts," added Paul Kepovitz, Co-founder and Head of Engineering. "ScopeGuard intercepts the retrieval process at the vector database level, guaranteeing that an LLM cannot reveal restricted information because it never sees it."

The $14M Series A funding will be used to accelerate product development, expand integrations with leading vector databases and identity providers, and build out ScopeGuard’s enterprise sales and support teams. 

**About ScopeGuard AI**
Founded in 2023 in San Francisco, ScopeGuard provides enterprise middleware that enforces zero-trust access controls for RAG pipelines. For more information, visit www.scopeguard.ai.
