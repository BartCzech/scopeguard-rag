ScopeGuard offers flexible, per-seat pricing designed to scale with your AI deployment. Choose the tier that best fits your organization's security needs.

### Starter Plan
Ideal for small teams deploying their first internal AI assistants.
* **$15 / user / month**
* Core scope-based retrieval middleware
* Support for up to 3 vector databases
* Pre-built identity integrations (Google Workspace, basic OAuth)
* Standard community support

### Business Plan
Built for mid-sized organizations requiring granular control and auditing.
* **$45 / user / month**
* Granular scope inheritance and nested policies
* Full audit log dashboard (30-day retention)
* Slack integration for real-time alerts
* Premium SSO integrations (Okta, Auth0)
* Priority email support

### Enterprise Plan
For large-scale, mission-critical AI deployments with strict compliance requirements.
* **Custom Pricing** (Contact Sales)
* Dedicated VPC deployment
* Unlimited vector database connections
* Custom embedding isolation
* 1-year audit log retention
* Dedicated Customer Success Manager
* 99.99% Uptime SLA
