Welcome to ScopeGuard AI. 

As enterprises rush to deploy AI assistants across their internal knowledge bases, a critical security flaw has emerged: LLMs are terrible at keeping secrets. When an employee asks an internal chatbot a question, they shouldn't be able to access the CEO's board presentations, HR's salary bands, or proprietary source code unless they are explicitly authorized. 

At ScopeGuard, our philosophy is simple: **Your AI knows only what it should.**

ScopeGuard is an enterprise middleware solution that sits directly between your AI assistant applications (the LLM) and your company’s vector databases. Rather than relying on easily bypassed system prompts to restrict an AI's output, ScopeGuard intercepts the retrieval process itself. 

**How it works:**
1. **Query Interception:** When a user prompts the AI, the query passes through ScopeGuard.
2. **Identity Verification:** ScopeGuard authenticates the user's identity and retrieves their specific access scopes.
3. **Scope-Filtered Retrieval:** ScopeGuard translates the query into a scope-aware vector search, ensuring that the database only returns document chunks the user is cleared to see.
4. **Safe Generation:** The LLM receives a curated, safe context window and generates an answer completely free of restricted data.

With seamless integrations for major identity providers (Okta, Auth0) and vector stores (Pinecone, Weaviate), ScopeGuard ensures your deployment is secure by design.
