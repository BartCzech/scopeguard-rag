Trust is the foundation of ScopeGuard AI. Below are answers to common questions regarding how we handle and protect your data.

**Are you SOC 2 Compliant?**
Security is our top priority. We are currently undergoing our SOC 2 Type II audit, with official certification expected by Q2 2025. In the interim, we can provide our completed SOC 2 Type I report to enterprise prospects under NDA.

**Where is my data stored?**
By default, all ScopeGuard infrastructure is hosted on AWS in the United States (us-east-1). To support our international customers, we are expanding our data center footprint to the EU (Frankfurt) in 2025.

**Does ScopeGuard train its own AI models on my data?**
Absolutely not. We are a middleware routing layer. We do not persist your vector embeddings, nor do we use your corporate data, queries, or logs to train any Large Language Models. Your data remains entirely your own.

**How is data encrypted?**
All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.2 or higher. Any metadata or configuration policies stored at rest within our infrastructure are encrypted using AES-256. 

**What happens if ScopeGuard goes down?**
We offer a 99.99% uptime SLA for our Enterprise tier. If our API becomes unreachable, our SDK defaults to a "fail-closed" state, meaning no documents are retrieved, preventing accidental data leakage during an outage.
